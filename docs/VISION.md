# Vision

Make capable, trustworthy business software available to anyone—from an
individual professional to an enterprise—without requiring them to surrender
their data, understand infrastructure, or assemble a pile of disconnected
tools.

dopOS-OSS is the open foundation for a local-first web workspace where people
can ask for work in plain language, understand the proposed outcome, approve
important changes, and retain a complete operational memory.
