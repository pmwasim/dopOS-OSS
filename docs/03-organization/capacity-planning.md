# Capacity Planning

## Purpose
Establish a reusable operating baseline for **Capacity Planning**. This document intentionally makes no company-specific claim.

## Scope
Applies when the accountable owner adopts this subject for [COMPANY_NAME].

## Operating baseline
The accountable owner assigns an operator, approver, reviewer, contributor, informed party, backup owner, and any permitted automated system or agent. Work must produce evidence, follow applicable higher-authority documents, and escalate unknown legal, tax, privacy, security, or contractual obligations.

## Controls and evidence
Use the relevant register, approval record, and linked process template. Record exceptions, risks, decisions, and unresolved gaps rather than assuming facts.

## Review requirements
Review annually and after a material change, incident, legal obligation, or owner decision.
