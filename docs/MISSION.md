# Mission

Build an open, self-hostable web platform that combines document work,
business workflows, scheduling, meetings, automation, and an explainable
assistant through open standards and open-source integrations.

The platform must remain useful offline or on a private network, truthful
about its capabilities, safe with privileged operations, and modular enough to
serve different sectors and organisation sizes.
